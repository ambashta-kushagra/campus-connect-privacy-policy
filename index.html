01 Scope & who this covers

Campus Connect is a campus access and anti-proxy attendance system built for Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur. It replaces manual attendance registers and gate logs with a rotating QR code that a student's phone generates, which guards scan at campus and hostel gates.

This policy explains what information the Campus Connect Android app and its backend collect, why, who can see it, and how long it's kept. It applies to students, guards, hostel wardens, and administrators who use the app as part of their role at the Institute.

Campus Connect is an institutional system issued to you as a member of MNIT — it is not a consumer app you can use independently of the Institute, and access requires an active MNIT identity.

04 App permissions

Camera

Used only by the guard role, to scan a student's QR code at a gate. Campus Connect does not use the camera for facial recognition — the guard confirms identity by comparing the person in front of them with the profile photo already on file.

Internet

Required for the app to talk to the Campus Connect backend — signing in, generating codes, submitting scans, and syncing records.

Notifications

Used to alert wardens to flagged records (e.g. a student who missed curfew) that need review.

Campus Connect does not request access to your contacts, SMS, call log, photos/media beyond what's needed to display your own profile photo, or precise/background location.

05 How we use your information

  • Operating the core system — verifying identity at sign-in, generating and validating rotating QR codes, recording gate and facility movement, and preventing a code from being reused (replay protection).
  • Preventing proxy attendance — the entire point of the device-binding and rotating-code design is to make it impractical for someone else to check a student in or out on their behalf.
  • Hostel and campus safety workflows — flagging missed curfews or unresolved exits so a warden can follow up, and letting students submit leave requests or disputes.
  • Administration — roster management, resetting a lost or replaced device, and generating aggregate reports (e.g. gate traffic load) for the Institute.
  • Security and troubleshooting — audit logs and operational logs described above.

We do not use your data for advertising, and Campus Connect contains no advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.

06 Who can see your data

Access is scoped by role — nobody sees more than their role needs:

Guards

See a student's name, ID, and photo at the moment of a scan, to confirm identity at the gate.

Wardens

See movement, flag, and leave records only for students in their own hostel — not campus-wide.

Administrators

Manage the roster and system configuration, and can access records and audit logs as needed to operate Campus Connect for the Institute.

You

Can see your own movement history, submit leave requests, and dispute a flagged record.

08 Retention

Movement, flag, and leave records are retained for [retention period — to be set by the Institute] to support hostel administration and safety review, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Account and roster data is retained for as long as you're an active member of the Institute, and removed after you leave in line with Institute record-keeping policy.

A rotating-code secret used to scan is discarded automatically shortly after use — it is not kept once a code has been checked.

09 Security

  • All traffic between the app and the backend is encrypted in transit.
  • QR codes rotate roughly every 30 seconds and cannot be reused once scanned — a screenshot or a reused code is rejected.
  • Each account is bound to a single device at a time; a lost or replaced phone requires an administrator to reset the binding.
  • Sign-in sessions use short-lived tokens that are rotated regularly rather than long-lived passwords stored on the device.
  • Access to student records is restricted by role, enforced on the backend rather than trusted to the app alone.

No system is perfectly secure, but Campus Connect is designed so that no single compromised device or screenshot is enough to falsify an attendance record.

10 Your rights & choices

  • Access — you can view your own movement history in the app at any time.
  • Correction / dispute — if a record looks wrong, you can raise a dispute in-app for your warden to review.
  • Device changes — if you lose or replace your phone, contact your administrator to have your device binding reset.
  • Questions about your data — reach out using the contact details below.

Because Campus Connect is an institutional access-control system tied to your enrollment, you cannot opt out of the core movement-logging function while using campus facilities that require a gate scan — this is the same principle as an existing physical gate register, digitized.

11 Request account & data deletion

Because Campus Connect is an institutional identity system — every account is created from the official MNIT roster, not self-registered — you can't delete your own account directly from within the app. This is intentional: your account is tied to your enrollment record, the same way a physical ID card is.

To request that your account and associated data (movement history, leave requests, device binding) be deleted, email [deletion request contact email] from your MNIT email address with your college ID and the request. This applies to:

  • Current students/staff who want their historical movement data removed while remaining on the roster — subject to the retention rules in Section 08 and any Institute record-keeping requirements for disciplinary or safety records.
  • Former students/staff whose enrollment has ended — accounts and associated data are deleted as part of the Institute's standard roster offboarding, and you may request this be expedited.

We'll confirm what was deleted and what (if anything) had to be retained for legal, safety, or audit reasons, and why.

12 Age & eligibility

Campus Connect is issued only to enrolled members of MNIT Jaipur (students, staff, and security personnel) via the official Institute roster, and sign-in requires an Institute-issued email account. It is not intended for, and is not directed at, children outside this context.
